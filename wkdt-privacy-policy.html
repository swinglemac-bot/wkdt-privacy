WKDT Cadet Radio — Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

WKDT Cadet Radio is the companion app for WKDT 89.3 FM, the cadet radio station at the United States Military Academy at West Point. The app is operated by the WKDT cadet club ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and where it is stored.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you register as a club member, you provide your name and email address, and you create a password. Sign-in is handled by our backend provider, Supabase Auth. Your password is stored only in hashed form by Supabase Auth; the app itself never stores your password.
  • Profile information. Your member profile includes your name, the club section(s) you belong to (for example DJs or Sportscasters), your role in the club (member, leadership, or admin), and your membership status.
  • Club activity data. When you use the app as a member, we store the things you do in it: event sign-ups, meeting attendance check-ins, and any announcements you post. This is the club's operational data — rosters, events, attendance, and announcements.
  • Listening. The live radio stream and now-playing information are served by the club's own streaming server (AzuraCast). As with any web stream, your device's IP address is visible to that server while you listen. We do not keep listening logs in the app.

Membership approval

WKDT is a closed club, so new accounts start as "pending." A club leader must approve an account before it becomes active, unless the person registering enters the club's access code, which is checked by our server and activates the account immediately. Pending accounts can see only their own profile — no roster, event, or attendance data.

Guest listening

You do not need an account to listen. Without signing in, the app only plays the live stream and shows now-playing information. Guests do not receive any member data. The only information a guest can send us is an event booking request, described next.

Event booking requests

Anyone — guest or member — can ask WKDT to DJ or broadcast an event using the "Book WKDT for an Event" form on the Listen tab. The form collects your name, email address, organization (if any), and details about the event: its type, date, location, and your description of what you need. You do not need an account to send a request. Requests are stored in the same database described above and are visible only to club leadership, who use the information solely to respond to you about the booking. If a request is accepted, leadership may add the event to the club's event list inside the app. Leadership can delete requests, and you can ask us to delete yours through the contact below.

How information is used

Information is used only to run the club's features in the app: showing the member roster, managing events and sign-ups, recording meeting attendance, posting announcements between members, and responding to event booking requests. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it with third parties for marketing, we do not show advertising, and the app contains no third-party analytics or tracking of any kind.

Where information is stored

Account, profile, and club activity data is stored with Supabase, our hosted database and sign-in provider, in the United States (Americas region). The radio stream itself is served by the club's AzuraCast server. The app contains no third-party software development kits other than the Supabase client used to connect to our own backend.

Who can see club data

The roster, event sign-ups, attendance records, and announcements are visible to other approved members of the club inside the app. Club leadership and admins can manage memberships, including approving new members and removing members.

Data retention and deletion

We keep member and club activity data while the account is active and the club is operating. A club admin can remove a member and their data. You can also request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us through our website (see Contact below), and we will delete it.

Children

The app is intended for members and supporters of a college cadet club and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete your account and data, can be sent to the WKDT cadet club through our website, wkdt89point3.com.
